Votica — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Votica ("the app", "we") is an online church and prayer app. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and why. Votica has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no AI processing.

1. Accounts

When you open Votica, an anonymous account is created for you automatically with Google Firebase Authentication, so your candles, flowers and intentions can be saved. No name or email is needed for this.

You may optionally sign in with Google to keep your data when you change or reinstall your phone. When you do, Firebase receives the account identifier, display name and email address provided by Google. Google may also process device and network information to authenticate you and protect its service.

2. Community prayer intentions

If you share a prayer intention, the text you write and the name you choose (optional; you can stay anonymous) are stored in our database and shown to other users of the app, together with the number of people praying for it. Please do not include phone numbers, email addresses, links or other personal or sensitive information about yourself or others. The app blocks links, phone numbers and emails and filters inappropriate words.

  • When you tap "I pray for this" or report an intention, a record linked to your account identifier is saved so the same person cannot count twice.
  • Intentions reported by many users are hidden automatically. Hiding a user is stored only on your device.
  • You can delete your own intentions at any time in the app.

3. Your saved data

To sync between devices, the app stores a private copy of your in-app data linked to your account: candles and their intentions, flowers offered, wishes, names and dates you add to the memorial wall, your avatar, and app settings such as language. Only you can read this data.

4. Presence and statistics

While the app is open, the time you were last active is stored so the app can show how many people are online. The app also keeps anonymous daily counters (for example, how many candles were lit today) that are not linked to you.

5. Permissions and links

Votica does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library. Photos you take with the in-app photo button are saved only to your device. Buttons for live-streamed Mass or daily readings open external websites in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

6. Service providers

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores the data described above. Google provides optional sign-in. These providers may process data outside your country under their privacy terms: Firebase, Google. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account and data → Delete permanently. This removes your shared intentions, synced data, presence record and account. Anonymous daily counters, and the "praying" and report markers you left on other people's intentions (a time filed under your former random account identifier), are kept. See also Delete your account. Depending on your region (for example GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data; email us to exercise them.

8. Children

Votica is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
