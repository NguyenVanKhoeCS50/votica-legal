Votica — Delete your account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Votica and tap Settings (gear icon, top right).
  2. Tap Delete account and data.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete permanently to confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email http.ccare@gmail.com with the subject "Votica account deletion request" from the Google account you used to sign in. We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (anonymous or Google sign-in link).
  • Prayer intentions you shared.
  • Synced data: candles, flowers, wishes, memorial wall names, avatar and settings.
  • Your online-presence record.

What is kept

Anonymous daily counters (for example, the number of candles lit on a day) contain no personal data and are kept. "Praying" and report markers you left on other people's intentions contain only a time, filed under the random account identifier that no longer exists, and remain so those counts stay correct. Google Firebase security logs and backups may remain for their standard retention periods.
